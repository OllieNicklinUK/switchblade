- VOICE engine: after [klattsch](https://github.com/tgies/klattsch) by Tony Gies (MIT); formant data from Klatt (1980) and Hillenbrand et al. (1995)
- PHYS engine: models ported from [DaisySP](https://github.com/electro-smith/DaisySP) by Electrosmith and Emilie Gillet ([MIT](LICENSES/MIT-DaisySP.txt)) and from Emilie Gillet's [eurorack](https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack) code ([MIT](LICENSES/MIT-Rings.txt))
- NANO (the ACID engine): its 303 voice is a port of [Open303](https://github.com/RobinSchmidt/Open303) by Robin Schmidt (RS-MET) ([MIT](LICENSES/MIT-Open303.txt)), by way of `bass303` from [X0X](https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-x0x) by [charlesvestal](https://github.com/charlesvestal) (GPL-3.0), itself after [schwung-303](https://github.com/charlesvestal/schwung-303) (GPL-3.0; Devilfish ranges after jc303 by midilab); its RAT drive model is from [dm-Rat](https://github.com/davemollen/dm-Rat) by Dave Mollen (GPL-3.0)
- DRIFT engine: the Juno voice is a float, no-libm port of the DSP of the [Ultramaster KR-106](https://github.com/kayrockscreenprinting/ultramaster_kr106) by Karl LaRocca (kayrockscreenprinting; after the original Ultramaster Group), GPL-3.0 ([notice](LICENSES/GPL-3-KR106.txt)): its four-pole OTA ladder (`kr106::VCF`), its polyBLEP DCO, the per-voice drift and variance, the 4-position HPF, and the half-band resampler (the coefficients of Laurent de Soras' HIIR library, WTFPL); JUNO's presets 33-50 are 18 of the KR-106's factory presets (its Juno-60 tape dump and Juno-106 factory SysEx, `Source/KR106_Presets_JUCE.h`; `tools/drift_kr106_presets.py`)
- NANO's SUB voice and its 30 presets, the per-track DIST (a drive) and the JUNO machine's chorus: written for Switch Blade (Ollie Nicklin, 2026; `fm1_subsynth.h`, `fm1_drive.h`, `fm1_juno_chorus.h`), GPL-3.0 with the rest
- CZ-Z machine (Switch Blade V2): the Casio CZ's sound chip, the NEC/Casio uPD933, ported from [MAME](https://github.com/mamedev/mame)'s `upd933.cpp` by Devin Acker ([BSD-3-Clause](LICENSES/BSD-3-MAME-upd933.txt)), held to it sample for sample (`tests/cz_ref.cpp`); its 8-step DCW envelope written for Switch Blade; its 50 presets converted from the Casio CZ-230S's tones as CZ-101 SysEx ([CZ-Presets](https://github.com/alphacharlie/CZ-Presets) by alphacharlie; Casio's factory data; `tools/czz_cz230s_presets.py`)
- JP-Z machine (Switch Blade V2): the Roland JP-8000 Super Saw, written for Switch Blade from the published analysis of the JP's ESP2 microcode by The Usual Suspects and [atosynth](https://atosynth.blogspot.com/2026/02/the-super-saw-code.html) (the detune law and knob curve, the mix), and Adam Szabó's *How to Emulate the Super Saw* (KTH, 2010); no Roland code. Its 50 factory presets are [Alan Marcero](https://alanmarcero.com)'s, converted from his free JP-8000 / JP-8080 bank (`jp80x0_rev_2`, `presets/jp/`; `tools/jpz_alan_presets.py`, `docs/jpz_presets.md`) with his permission (2026-11-19) — thank you, Alan
- GW-Z machine (Switch Blade V2): its three models are [Quantum GammaWave](https://github.com/Guillel51/Quantum-GammaWave)'s WHEEL (ORGAN), VOICE (VOX) and ADDTV (ADD) engines by Leo Kuroshita (Hügelton Instruments) and Guillel51 (GPL-3.0-only), ported to Switch Blade's machine contract, with many of their presets; VOICE after Klatt (1980) and klattsch by Tony Gies (design reference), formant data from Hillenbrand et al. (1995)
- MICRO machine (Switch Blade V2): after the Waldorf Microwave I, written for Switch Blade; its wavetables are our own PPG-style ones (`tools/gen_mw_tables.py`), not PPG's or Waldorf's
- Switch Blade V2, from [Quantum GammaWave](https://github.com/Guillel51/Quantum-GammaWave) by Guillel51 (a Felucca firmware for the FM-1, GPL-3.0-only): the flash identification that lets the newer FM-1 flash save (`hal/fm1_flash_id.h`, `tests/flash_id_test.c`), the key-scan fast path (`hal/fm1_input.h`), the load-based voice limit (`audio.c shed_check`, `voice.c load_tight`), and the preset audit and level report (`tests/preset_audit_test.c`, `tools/gen_preset_levels.py`), each adapted to Switch Blade
- FM6 engine: msfa from [Dexed](https://github.com/asb2m10/dexed) by Google Inc. and Pascal Gauthier ([Apache-2.0](LICENSES/Apache-2.0-msfa.txt))
- Browser emulator: after [X0X](https://github.com/charlesvestal/fm1-x0x) by [charlesvestal](https://github.com/charlesvestal) (GPL-3.0), a Felucca fork whose browser build showed the way
